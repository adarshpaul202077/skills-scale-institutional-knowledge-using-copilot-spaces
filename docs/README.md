## Project Management Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a five-phase lifecycle:

| Phase | Purpose | Key Activities |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| **1. Initiation** | Validate business need, align stakeholders | Problem statement, one-pager, go/no-go decision |
| **2. Planning** | Break work into deliverables, estimate, identify risks | Backlog creation, estimation, milestone planning |
| **3. Execution** | Daily delivery, testing, collaboration | Standups, PRs, testing, risk monitoring |
| **4. Release** | Deploy to production with confidence | Pre-release checks, deployment, verification |
| **5. Close & Retrospective** | Capture learnings and drive improvement | Retrospectives, action items, metrics review |

## Key Artifacts

Each OctoAcme project maintains the following artifacts throughout its lifecycle:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** – Problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline
- **Prioritized Backlog** – Backlog items with acceptance criteria, estimates, and priorities
- **Definition of Done (DoD)** – Clear criteria for what "done" means for the team
- **Risk Register** – Tracked risks with ID, description, impact, likelihood, owner, and mitigation plan
- **Release Plan** – Milestones, release schedule, and integration points
- **Retrospective Notes** – Learnings, action items, and process improvements
- **Metrics & Dashboards** – Velocity, burndown, success metrics, error rates, latency

## Communication Cadence

OctoAcme teams maintain consistent communication through:

| Cadence | Purpose | Participants |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| **Daily Standups (15 min)** | Progress, blockers, dependencies | Delivery team |
| **Weekly PM + PdM Sync** | Alignment on priorities, risks, dependencies | PM, Product Manager, Product Lead |
| **Twice-Weekly Delivery Standup** | Deep dive on execution | Delivery team (optional, as agreed) |
| **Weekly Stakeholder Updates** | Status, risks, decisions needed | Project stakeholders |
| **Sprint/Milestone Demos** | Show progress, gather feedback | Team + stakeholders |
| **Sprint/Milestone Retrospectives** | Capture learnings, identify improvements | Delivery team |
| **Ad-Hoc Escalations** | Address blockers and critical issues | Relevant stakeholders |

## Core Roles at a Glance

**Project Manager (PM)**
- Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, tracks risks and communications
- Ensures consistent documentation and stakeholder alignment

**Product Manager (PdM)**
- Defines outcomes, prioritizes work, measures success
- Validates solutions through research and metrics

**Developers**
- Implement features, write tests, participate in reviews
- Collaborate on design and identify technical risks

**QA/Testing**
- Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- Support integration and smoke testing

**Stakeholders**
- Provide inputs, approvals, and strategic guidance
- Help identify business priorities and constraints

See [Roles & Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) for detailed responsibilities and communication patterns.
